# CorbaTales — Physical Keepsake Book Design

> Product: $24.99 (+ Shipping &amp; Handling) hardcover of a child's 10 favorite stories
> Brand Guidelines v1.0 | July 2026

---

## 1. Book Specifications

**Format:** Hardcover, 8.5" × 8.5" (square — perfect for illustrated children's books)  
**Pages:** 32 pages (10 stories × 2 spreads each + cover/title/copyright)  
**Paper:** Premium 120gsm matte art paper with subtle cream tint  
**Cover:** Hardback with matte lamination, spot UV gloss on title and moon icon  
**Binding:** Case-bound with rounded spine  
**Ribbon marker:** Gold silk ribbon bookmark sewn into spine  

---

## 2. Cover Design

### Front Cover
- **Background:** Deep Midnight Blue (#1B1B3A) gradient with subtle star pattern
- **Title:** "My CorbaTales Storybook" in Playfair Display Bold, Warm Golden Amber (#F5A623), letter-spaced +0.05em
- **Subtitle:** "10 Bedtime Adventures" in Nunito Regular, Warm Cream (#FFF8F0), size 14pt
- **Illustration area:** Central framed artwork — a cozy bedroom with moonlight streaming through a window onto an open storybook, with a crescent moon and stars visible outside. Soft watercolor illustration style.
- **Child's name area:** A gold foil-style banner at the bottom: "Stories for [Child's Name]" — customized per book
- **Logo:** CorbaTales storybook icon (small, bottom-right corner)

### Back Cover
- **Background:** Deep Midnight Blue (#1B1B3A)
- **Tagline:** "Every night, a new story." in Nunito Regular, Warm Cream, centered
- **Decorative element:** Subtle gold star pattern at 15% opacity
- **Barcode area:** Bottom-right, white box with rounded corners
- **ISBN placeholder:** Bottom-left

### Spine
- **Width:** ~0.5" (32 pages × ~0.4mm paper thickness)
- **Text:** "CorbaTales · [Child's Name]" in Playfair Display Bold, gold foil effect, reading top-to-bottom

### Inside Flaps
- **Front flap:** Brief product description, "Record 3 minutes once. Every night, a new story in your voice."
- **Back flap:** "Create your keepsake at corbatales.com" with QR code

## 3. Interior Layout Template

### Page Structure
Each story occupies a 4-page spread (2 double-page spreads):

#### Spread 1: Title + Full Illustration (Pages 2-3)
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Full-bleed illustration spanning   │
│  both pages (watercolor scene       │
│  setting the story's mood)          │
│                                     │
│           Story Title ──────────    │
│           in Playfair Display Bold  │
│           28pt, Golden Amber        │
│                                     │
│    "Dedicated to [child's name]"    │
│    Nunito Italic, 12pt, Cream       │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Spread 2: Story Content (Pages 4-5)
```
┌─────────────────────┬─────────────────────┐
│  LEFT PAGE          │  RIGHT PAGE          │
│                     │                      │
│  ┌─────────────┐    │  Story text continues│
│  │ Illustration │    │                      │
│  │ (spot art)   │    │  in Nunito Regular   │
│  └─────────────┘    │  16pt, 1.6 line ht   │
│                     │                      │
│  Story text begins  │  Warm Cream (#FFF8F0)│
│  here...            │  on Midnight Blue    │
│                     │  background          │
│                     │                      │
│  Page 4             │  Page 5              │
│  [story icon] ◍     │  [story icon] ◍      │
└─────────────────────┴─────────────────────┘
```

### Typography (Interior)

| Element | Font | Size | Color | Leading |
|---------|------|------|-------|---------|
| Story Title | Playfair Display Bold | 28pt | #F5A623 | 32pt |
| Chapter Number | Playfair Display Regular | 72pt | #F5A623 (15% opacity) | — |
| Body Text | Nunito Regular | 16pt | #FFF8F0 | 26pt (1.6) |
| Page Number | Nunito Semi-Bold | 10pt | #C4A1E0 | — |
| Drop Cap (first letter) | Playfair Display Bold | 36pt | #F5A623 | — |
| Story Metadata (date/duration) | Inter Regular | 9pt | #C4A1E0 | — |

### Page Numbering
- Each page numbered at bottom center
- Story icon (small star crescent) to the left of page number on left pages, right on right pages
- Number format: "◍ 12" / "13 ◍"
- Color: Soft Lavender (#C4A1E0) at 50% opacity

### Chapter/Story Headings
- Large ornamental drop cap for the first letter of each story
- Story number in Roman numerals or small ordinal: "Story the First" / "Story the Second" etc.
- Each story heading includes a small decorative divider: a row of 3 stars (★ ★ ★) in Golden Amber

### Margins & Bleed
- **Bleed:** 0.125" on all sides
- **Inner margin (gutter):** 0.75"
- **Outer margin:** 0.5"
- **Top margin:** 0.5"
- **Bottom margin:** 0.5"

### Story: "The Brave Little Star"

Body text:

>
> One night, a little boy named Leo looked up at the sky and whispered...
