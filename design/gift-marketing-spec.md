### Gift Card Design (Digital Delivery)

**Email gift card design:**
- **Background:** Warm midnight blue gradient with animated gold stars
- **Header:** "You've received the gift of bedtime stories!" in Playfair Display Bold
- **Centerpiece:** CorbaTales storybook icon (large, with glow effect) surrounded by floating sparkles
- **Message area:** "Dear [Parent Name], I've gifted you and [Child Name] a year of magical bedtime stories. Just click below, record your voice, and every night a new story awaits. With love, [Grandparent Name] ❤️"
- **CTA button:** "Claim Your Gift →" in Warm Golden Amber on midnight blue
- **Footer:** CorbaTales logo + "Questions? hello@corbatales.com"

### Gift Card Physical (Optional Mailer)
- **Format:** 5" × 7" folded card, premium matte finish
- **Front:** "A Year of Bedtime Stories" in gold foil
- **Inside:** Personalized message from grandparent + setup instructions
- **Who it's for:** Sold as an add-on ($4.99) for those who want a physical card
