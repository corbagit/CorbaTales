## 1. Core Illustration Style

### Artistic Direction
- **Medium:** Soft watercolor meets digital art — painterly but clean
- **Texture:** Subtle paper grain overlay for a printed storybook feel
- **Line work:** Gentle, rounded, hand-drawn quality — no harsh straight lines or sharp edges
- **Lighting:** Warm golden glows, soft moonlight, gentle rim lighting
- **Color treatment:** Slightly desaturated for a dreamy, bedtime-appropriate atmosphere

### Color Palette for Illustrations
The illustration palette is warmer and softer than the brand palette:

| Color Role | Hex | Usage |
|-----------|-----|-------|
| Moonlight glow | `#FFF8F0` | Highlighting, magical light sources |
| Warm amber | `#F5A623` | Fireflies, candles, magic sparkles, sunsets |
| Soft lavender | `#C4A1E0` | Dreamy skies, magical mist, fantasy elements |
| Midnight blue | `#1B1B3A` | Night skies, deep water, shadows |
| Teal | `#4ECDC4` | Forests, magical pools, friendly creatures |
| Warm coral | `#FF8A80` | Sunrises, warm cheeks, cozy interiors |
| Soft sage | `#A8C5B0` | Natural elements, gentle landscapes |

### Character Design Principles
- **Proportions:** Child characters have 1:1.2 head-to-body ratio (big head, small body = cute)
- **Eyes:** Large, expressive, round — 30-40% of face height
- **Faces:** Soft cheeks with warm coral blush
- **Animal characters:** Stylized anthropomorphic — friendly, never scary
- **Poses:** Gentle, relaxed, often reading, sleeping, exploring
- **Diversity:** Characters of all skin tones, abilities, and family structures

### Environmental Design
- **Composition:** Centered subject with atmospheric background
- **Depth:** 3 layers — foreground (detailed elements), midground (subject), background (atmosphere)
- **Light sources:** Always present — moonlight, candlelight, fairy lights, window light
- **Weather/sky:** Starry nights, soft clouds, gentle rain, never storms or darkness

## 2. Story Theme — Illustration Reference Cards

Each theme has specific visual elements, color direction, and example prompts.

### 2.1 Adventure & Exploration 🌲

**Vibe:** Wondrous, exciting but never scary, friendly forests, treasure maps

**Color direction:** Teal + Warm Amber + Forest Green  
**Time of day:** Golden hour or twilight  
**Key elements:** Maps, compasses, friendly forests, gentle rivers, treasure chests

**Example prompt:**
```
A children's book illustration in soft watercolor style. A child wearing a tiny backpack 
exploring a friendly forest with glowing mushrooms and a cute fox companion. Warm golden 
sunlight filtering through the trees. Soft teal and amber colors. Gentle, rounded lines. 
Cozy adventurous feeling. No scary elements.
```

## 3. Illustration Composition Templates

### 3.1 Full-Page Spread (Landscape)
- **Usage:** Story introduction, magical moments, scene setting
- **Aspect ratio:** ~16:9 within the book
- **Composition:** Subject off-center (rule of thirds), atmospheric background

### 3.2 Half-Page with Text (Portrait)
- **Usage:** Story progression, dialogue moments
- **Aspect ratio:** 4:5
- **Composition:** Illustration on top 60%, text on bottom 40%
- **Integration:** Soft gradient fade at the bottom of the illustration for text overlay

### 3.3 Spot Illustration (Small)
- **Usage:** Decorative elements, chapter breaks, emphasis on objects
- **Size:** 25-30% of page
- **Composition:** Single character or object with simple background

### 3.4 Vignette (Framed)
- **Usage:** Tender moments, close-ups, emotional beats
- **Composition:** Soft circular or oval framing, vignette darkening at edges
- **Lighting:** Warm spotlight effect on the subject

## 4. Image Generation Prompt Templates

### Master Prompt Template
```
A children's book illustration in soft watercolor digital art style.
[SUBJECT DESCRIPTION] in a [SETTING/ENVIRONMENT].
[LIGHTING/MOOD].
Color palette: [PRIMARY COLORS].
[CHARACTER TRAITS: large expressive eyes, rounded shapes, soft features].
[SPECIFIC ELEMENTS].
Gentle, warm, magical atmosphere. 
Children's book illustration style, safe for ages 2-10.
```

## 5. Quality Check — What to Avoid

### ❌ NEVER generate these:
- Scary or threatening expressions on any character
- Dark, menacing environments (no dark dungeons, haunted houses, storms)
- Sharp teeth, claws, weapons, or violence
- Characters crying in distress or fear
- Lonely or abandoned children
- Realistic or photorealistic styles
- Cold, sterile, or overly corporate art styles
- Abstract or surreal art that could confuse a child

### ✅ ALWAYS include:
- Warm lighting source (moon, lamp, sun, fireflies, stars)
- Soft, rounded shapes throughout
- Large, expressive eyes on characters
- A cozy or wondrous atmosphere
- Age-appropriate scenes (ages 2-10)
- Clear, readable compositions

## 6. Character Consistency Guide

For recurring characters across multiple illustrations in the same story:

- **Color palette consistency:** Same character always uses the same colors
- **Proportions:** Maintain head-to-body ratio across illustrations
- **Facial features:** Eyes, nose, mouth style consistent
- **Outfit/accessories:** Same visual elements (a red scarf, blue bow, etc.)

### Prompt for Character Consistency
```
[CHARACTER NAME] is a child with [skin tone] skin, [hair color] curly hair, 
wearing a [color] sweater. Large round expressive [eye color] eyes, 
soft round cheeks. [CHARACTER TRAITS].
```

## 8. Integration Notes for the AI/ML Engineer

When generating illustrations for a story:

1. **First determine the theme** based on the story content (adventure, animals, bedtime, etc.)
2. **Select the composition template** based on the story beat (full-page for scene-setting, half-page for dialogue, spot for decorative, vignette for emotional moments)
3. **Build the prompt** using the Master Prompt Template + Theme-Specific additions + character consistency details
4. **Generate at the appropriate aspect ratio** for the book format
5. **Apply post-processing** if needed (brighten for readability, add soft vignette)

### Suggested Prompt Assembly Function
```
function buildIllustrationPrompt(theme, subject, setting, characters) {
  return `A children's book illustration in soft watercolor digital art style. 
${subject} in ${setting}. ${getThemeLighting(theme)}. 
Color palette: ${getThemeColors(theme)}. 
${characters.map(c => `${c.name} has ${c.appearance}`).join('. ')}.
${getThemeElements(theme)}. 
Gentle, warm, magical atmosphere. Children's book illustration, safe for ages 2-10.`;
}
```
