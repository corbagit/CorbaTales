## 1. Brand Essence

CorbaTales creates personalized, AI-generated bedtime stories narrated in a parent's own voice. Every night, a brand-new tale with original illustrations — a magical keepsake that grows with your child.

**Brand personality:** Warm, magical, trustworthy, nurturing, wonder-filled  
**Tone of voice:** Gentle, reassuring, imaginative, parent-friendly  
**Target audience:** Parents of children ages 2–10 who want a consistent, special bedtime ritual  
**Core emotion:** The cozy feeling of being tucked in — safety, love, and the magic of storytelling

## 2. Logo

### Primary Logo
The CorbaTales logo combines a storybook icon (an open book with a glowing crescent moon rising from its pages) with the wordmark in custom lettering.

- **Icon:** An open book with a crescent moon + single star emerging from the pages, rendered in warm golden amber (#F5A623) against deep midnight blue (#1B1B3A)
- **Wordmark:** "CorbaTales" set in Playfair Display Bold, with delicate swash alternates on the "C" and "T"
- **Tagline (optional):** "Every night, a new story." set in Nunito Regular, letter-spaced +0.05em

### Logo Variations
1. **Full logo** — Icon + wordmark + tagline (for website header, print)
2. **Icon + wordmark** — Icon + "CorbaTales" (for app header, social media)
3. **Icon only** — The storybook-moon icon (for favicon, app icon, avatar)

### Logo Clear Space
Maintain clear space equal to the height of the letter "C" on all sides of the logo.

### Logo Do's and Don'ts
- ✅ Use on deep midnight blue, warm cream, or white backgrounds
- ✅ Use the icon standalone for small spaces
- ❌ Never stretch or distort
- ❌ Never change colors
- ❌ Never place on busy patterned backgrounds
- ❌ Never add drop shadows or effects

## 4. Typography

### Headings: Playfair Display
A classic, elegant serif typeface with a storybook quality.

| Level | Weight | Size | Line Height | Usage |
|-------|--------|------|-------------|-------|
| H1 | Bold (700) | 48px / 3rem | 1.1 | Hero headlines, page titles |
| H2 | Bold (700) | 36px / 2.25rem | 1.2 | Section headings |
| H3 | Semi-Bold (600) | 28px / 1.75rem | 1.3 | Card headings, sub-sections |
| H4 | Semi-Bold (600) | 22px / 1.375rem | 1.4 | Minor headings |

### Body: Nunito
A rounded, friendly, highly-readable sans-serif.

| Size | Weight | Line Height | Usage |
|------|--------|-------------|-------|
| 18px / 1.125rem | Regular (400) | 1.6 | Body text, story content |
| 16px / 1rem | Regular (400) | 1.5 | Secondary text, cards |
| 14px / 0.875rem | Semi-Bold (600) | 1.4 | Labels, small text |

### UI: Inter
A clean, modern sans-serif for interface elements.

| Size | Weight | Usage |
|------|--------|-------|
| 16px | Medium (500) | Navigation links |
| 14px | Medium (500) | Button text |
| 13px | Regular (400) | Form labels, metadata |
| 12px | Regular (400) | Captions, timestamps |

### Typography Rules
- Headings use Playfair Display — never substitute a different serif
- Body copy uses Nunito for warmth and readability
- UI elements use Inter for clarity and professionalism
- Minimum font size: 12px (captions only)
- Maximum line length for body text: 70 characters
- Story text within the reader: 18px Nunito, 1.8 line height for optimal readability

- Use Feather icons or custom-drawn storybook-style icons
- Line weight: 2px
- Default color: inherit from text color

## 6. Illustration Style

CorbaTales illustrations follow a **soft watercolor meets digital art** style:

- **Color palette:** Primarily warm tones (amber, cream, lavender) with midnight blue accents
- **Line work:** Gentle, rounded, hand-drawn feel — no harsh straight lines
- **Characters:** Soft, stylized animals and children with large expressive eyes
- **Environments:** Cozy bedrooms, moonlit forests, starry skies, magical landscapes
- **Lighting:** Warm golden glows, soft moonlight, gentle gradients
- **Texture:** Subtle paper texture overlay for a storybook feel

### Key Illustration Elements
1. **The Moon** — A crescent moon with a soft golden glow (brand icon element)
2. **Stars** — Five-pointed gold stars scattered throughout
3. **Storybook** — An open book with pages glowing
4. **Cozy Bedroom** — Warm blankets, pillows, stuffed animals
5. **Magical Portal** — Book pages transforming into a story world

## 7. UI Mockups (Screen Descriptions)

### 7.1 Landing Page
- **Hero:** Full-width gradient background (midnight blue to deep purple) with floating golden stars
- **Logo** centered top, navigation right-aligned
- **Headline:** "Every night, a brand-new story — in your voice." in Playfair Display Bold
- **Subheadline:** "Record 3 minutes once. Answer a few questions. Each night the AI creates a fresh story personalized just for them, with original illustrations."
- **CTA Button:** "Start Your Free Trial" — Warm Golden Amber
- **Visual:** A tablet/mockup showing a story being read, with floating storybook elements
- **Features section:** 3-column grid with icons (Voice Magic, New Story Daily, Illustrated Keepsakes)
- **How it works:** 3-step numbered flow with simple illustrations
- **Testimonials:** Carousel of parent quotes
- **Pricing:** Two cards (Monthly $12.99 / Annual $99.99)
- **Footer:** Deep Midnight Blue, links, social icons

### 7.2 Dashboard (Parent Home)
- **Header:** Logo left, nav (My Stories, Library, Settings, Profile icon) right
- **Welcome section:** "Good evening, [Name]" — Playfair Display, with a "Tonight's Story" CTA card
- **Story of the Night:** Large card with illustration thumbnail, "Tonight's Story" badge, title, "Listen Now" button
- **Recent Stories:** Horizontal scroll of thumbnail cards (3-5 recent stories)
- **Quick Stats:** Stories count, nights streak, "Your child's favorite character" widget
- **Voice Status:** Badge showing voice recording status (✓ Recorded or "Record Your Voice" prompt)

### 7.3 Story Reader
- **Full-screen immersive reader mode**
- **Background:** Deep Midnight Blue gradient with subtle floating star particles
- **Story text:** 18px Nunito Regular, Warm Cream (#FFF8F0), 1.8 line height, centered text block
- **Illustration:** Full-width illustration above or beside text (responsive)
- **Audio controls:** Play/Pause, progress bar (Warm Golden Amber), skip 15s, speed control
- **Page turn:** Soft swipe or tap to advance, with a subtle page-turn animation
- **Navigation:** Back to library, bookmark icon, font-size controls
- **Narration indicator:** "Narrated by Mom/Dad" badge with a small voice-wave icon

### 7.4 Voice Recording Flow
- **Step 1: Introduction** — "Let's capture your voice so every story feels like you're reading it." Friendly illustration of a parent reading
- **Step 2: Sample Text** — A warm, 3-minute passage to read aloud (a classic children's poem or a CorbaTales sample story)
- **Step 3: Recording** — Large red record button, waveform visualization, timer (0:00 / 3:00)
- **Step 4: Review** — Playback with waveform, re-record option, "Sounds Great!" confirmation
- **Step 5: Processing** — Animated star/magic wand loading state "We're learning your voice..."
- **Step 6: Complete** — Celebration animation, confetti, "Your voice is ready! Tonight's story awaits."
- **Design notes:** Calming tones, reassuring copy, progress indicator at top showing steps

### 7.5 Library Page
- **Header:** "My Story Library" — Playfair Display
- **Filters:** Sort by date, favorite, or title — pill-shaped filter tags in Soft Lavender
- **Grid view:** 3-column grid of story cards
- **Story Card:** Illustration thumbnail (rounded corners 16px), title (Nunito Semi-Bold), date, "Play" button overlay on hover, bookmark icon top-right
- **Empty state:** "Your library is full of stories waiting to be told!" with whimsical illustration of an empty bookshelf
- **Search:** Search bar with magnifying glass icon, filters as you type
- **Pagination:** Simple "Load More" button or infinite scroll

### 7.6 Subscription & Settings
- **Plan Info:** Current plan card (Monthly/Annual), next billing date
- **Upgrade/Pricing:** Quick toggle between Monthly and Annual with savings highlighted
- **Child Profile:** Name, age, interests (tags like "Dragons 🐉", "Space 🚀", "Princesses 👑")
- **Voice Recordings:** List of voice profiles (parents can record multiple)
- **App Settings:** Notification preferences, bedtime reminder time, font size, theme toggle

## 9. Motion & Animation

- **Transitions:** 300ms ease-in-out for UI interactions
- **Page transitions:** Subtle fade + slide up (400ms)
- **Story page turn:** Gentle page curl animation (500ms)
- **Magic moments:** Stars shimmer with a pulsing glow (2s loop)
- **Loading states:** A crescent moon that gently rocks back and forth
- **Hover effects:** Cards lift 2px with shadow deepening (200ms)
- **Reduce motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` — disable all non-essential animations

---

## 10. Accessibility

- **Color contrast:** All text combinations meet WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text)
- **Focus indicators:** 2px solid Warm Golden Amber outline with 4px offset
- **Touch targets:** Minimum 44x44px for interactive elements
- **Text sizing:** Support browser font-size zoom up to 200%
- **Screen readers:** All icons have `aria-label`, all images have descriptive `alt` text
- **Motion:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for all animations
